# Live Operations Workflow

## Purpose

Live operations kept the platform running, understandable, and responsive to players over a long lifecycle. The work combined technical troubleshooting, content updates, community communication, support, and iteration.

## Operational Goals

- Keep the platform accessible and stable.
- Communicate updates clearly to players.
- Support players who run into setup, account, or gameplay issues.
- Ship seasonal and feature updates without confusing the community.
- Monitor recurring bugs or friction points.
- Maintain website, server, and community channels.

## Workflow

1. Identify a need, bug, event, or player-facing improvement.
2. Define the scope of the update.
3. Build or configure the change in a test-safe way.
4. Review possible player impact and edge cases.
5. Publish the update or event.
6. Communicate the change through website, Discord/community channels, or patch notes.
7. Monitor player feedback and support requests.
8. Fix issues, clarify instructions, or adjust balance as needed.

## Common Workstreams

- Website updates and public information pages.
- Community announcements and support responses.
- Seasonal content and event rewards.
- Bug fixes and player-facing troubleshooting.
- Gameplay balance adjustments.
- Feature documentation and installation instructions.
- Contributor coordination for community support.

## Support Thinking

Many issues are not just code problems. A live platform also needs clear communication, practical documentation, and user support. For example, if players repeatedly struggle with installation or a system interaction, the solution may involve better instructions, UI feedback, workflow changes, or a technical fix.

## What This Demonstrates

- Long-term ownership of a live technical platform.
- Technical support and communication under real user conditions.
- Release and update thinking.
- Community management and stakeholder support.
- Practical troubleshooting across website, server, client, and user workflows.
